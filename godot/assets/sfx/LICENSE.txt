Sound effects in this folder are short clips cut from two CC0 (public domain) libraries by tools/cut_sfx.py
(first shot of a recording, mono, pitched, faded, peak-normalised). Both are CC0: no attribution required.

1. The Free Firearm Sound Library
   https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
   Licence on the page (read 16 Oct 2026): "CC0" - "Our team holds CC0 NO RIGHTS RESERVED for this library.
   It may be used without royalty or credit". Recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and
   Matthew Nanney.
   Used for: shot_pistol (Walther PPQ), shot_smg / shot_ripper (PPSh), shot_minigun / shot_bullet_storm
   (AK-47), shot_shotgun (Mossberg), shot_railgun / shot_hand_cannon / shot_annihilator (Savage 10 .300
   Blackout), shot_grenade / shot_scattergun / shot_cluster (Model 12).

2. Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0, www.kenney.nl
   https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds - License.txt in the pack: "Creative Commons Zero, CC0".
   Used for: shot_laser (laserSmall), shot_prism (laserLarge), shot_rocket / shot_swarm / shot_flamethrower /
   shot_inferno (thrusterFire), explode (explosionCrunch).
